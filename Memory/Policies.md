# Policies

- Deposit: $25 non-refundable deposit at booking; the balance is paid after the job is complete. Never full payment upfront.
- Special orders: the customer buys the mount (or fireplace) before booking, with no deposit; or pays 50% of the total job upfront if Mount Atlanta orders it.
- Arrival window: 2 hours.
- Late cancels and no-shows: the deposit is not refunded.
- Warranty: 90 days on Mount Atlanta's work.
- Wall types: all wall types. Survey the job on arrival and explain to the customer if something can't be done.
- Travel: a travel fee may apply for far cities (e.g. Conyers, McDonough, Gainesville, Cumming, Cartersville, past Rome).
- Callbacks: call customers back within 30 minutes after a handoff.
- Customer contacts are saved in Ricardo's phone with a "CX" prefix on the name.
